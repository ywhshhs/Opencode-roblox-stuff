## Repeated Failed Approach

**Trigger:** The same tool, command, or implementation approach fails two or more times
**Outcome:** Oracle should intervene with concrete redirect guidance
**Root Cause:** Retrying without new information increases loop risk
**Successful Fix:** Pause, inspect current state, and change tactic based on evidence
**Prevention:** Explorer or Basher verifies the relevant state before another attempt
**Confidence:** 96%
**Observed:** oracle_prompt.md
