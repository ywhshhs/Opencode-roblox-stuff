✦ WYD is an internet‑slang acronym that most commonly means “What (are) you doing?” – a quick way to ask someone what they’re up to, similar to “What’s up?”

  Less‑common meanings also exist:

  • What would you do? – used as a rhetorical or hypothetical question.
  • World Youth Day – the Catholic Church’s international youth event held every few years.

  So, when you see “WYD” in a chat or text, the intended meaning is almost always “What are you doing?” unless the context clearly points to one of the alternative
